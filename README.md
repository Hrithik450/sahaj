# Sahaj

Sahaj is an accessibility-first hackathon project that makes **government** and **banking** digital services easier to understand and use.

It combines **plain-language AI**, **voice guidance**, **OCR for notices**, and **multilingual support** to help users complete important tasks with more confidence.

## Problem Statement

Millions of people in India face digital exclusion because of visual, hearing, motor, or cognitive impairments, language barriers, and low digital literacy. Most digital platforms are designed for an "average" user, making essential tasks like accessing services, filling forms, and understanding financial or government information difficult or inaccessible.

## Our Solution

Sahaj adds an adaptive accessibility layer on top of existing government and banking workflows instead of rebuilding them from scratch. It simplifies dense text, provides voice guidance and live captions, uses OCR to help auto-fill forms, and offers a conversational assistant that answers questions in plain language. With secure authentication, multilingual support, and offline-friendly flows, Sahaj makes digital services more inclusive and usable for underserved communities.

## What it does

### Government Features

- Simplifies government notices and letters into clear next steps
- Helps fill government forms one field at a time with guided support
- Finds the right public service, required documents, and next action
- Offers a voice companion for questions in plain language
- Includes a safe practice mode for trying tasks with confidence

### Banking Features

- Simplifies bank letters, EMI notices, and KYC messages
- Guides users through banking tasks like KYC updates and fraud reporting
- Explains SMS alerts and transactions in simple language
- Offers a voice companion for banking help on screen and in audio
- Includes a safe practice mode for flows like UPI transfers and submissions

### Shared Accessibility Features

- Supports English, Hindi, and Kannada
- Adapts the experience using accessibility preferences

## Why it matters

Many users struggle with forms, notices, banking alerts, and service portals. Sahaj reduces that friction by turning complex flows into guided, accessible experiences.
